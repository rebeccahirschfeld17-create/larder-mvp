Retainer Tiers

Monthly plans for premium and multi-location restaurant groups, based on complexity and how much execution Larder owns.

Monitor

Under $7,500/mo

For smaller premium operators or early relationships where Larder watches the vendor base and builds the first cost ledger.

  • Contract and renewal calendar
  • Document intake and baseline cost ledger
  • Invoice drift and benchmark alerts
  • Quarterly executive review
Move up when: multiple locations, recurring vendor actions, or monthly operator review becomes necessary.

Control

$7,500-$14,999/mo

The default tier for multi-location restaurant groups with enough complexity to need monthly vendor cost control.

  • Everything in Monitor
  • Monthly cost ledger updates
  • Location coverage by vendor category
  • Approval-ready vendor actions
  • Monthly executive review
Move up when: Larder is actively managing vendor outreach, RFPs, or renegotiation follow-up.

Managed

$15,000+/mo

For larger groups where Larder operates as the external vendor cost control desk across locations and categories.

  • Everything in Control
  • Vendor outreach and response tracking
  • RFP and renegotiation management
  • Relationship-sensitive approval previews
  • Savings verification after implementation
Best fit: premium or multi-location groups with shared vendors, fragmented rate cards, and active renewal risk.

How Larder finds the savings
The client sees only what matters
$492K found
Larder calculates true all-in vendor costs, checks contract leverage, compares better available terms, and turns each savings opportunity into a managed action.

True all-in cost

Quoted pricing becomes real cost after fees, rebates, minimums, delivery charges, replacement fees, and volume.

Contract leverage

Larder checks renewal dates, auto-renewal windows, notice deadlines, escalation language, rebate terms, and exclusivity.

Better available terms

Current pricing is checked against cleaner restaurant terms: lower processor rates, capped escalation, corrected pickup schedules, and tighter order guides.
